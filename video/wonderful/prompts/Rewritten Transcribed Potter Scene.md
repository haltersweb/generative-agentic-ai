POTTER
(potter's hand enters screen tapping at iPad on the desk which has the contract on it.)
I want you to manage my properties. Three hundred thousand a year.  Three-year contract. Starting Monday.

(George picks up iPad with the job offer on it, reading it incredulously for about 2 seconds.)

GEORGE
(flabbergasted, talking to himself as he looks at the iPad.)
Three hun... Three hundred thousand a year?

POTTER
You wouldn't mind living in the nicest house in town. Your wife in clothes she didn't order off a screen. Real trips, two a year. You wouldn't mind that, would you, George?

GEORGE
(incredulously but with a slight smile) Would I?
(looking around ironically as if he finds it unbelievable that Potter is offering this to him) You're not talking to somebody else around here, are you? (pointing at himself, maintaining disbelief.) You know, (raising his voice in disbelief and wonder) this is me, you remember me? George Bailey.

POTTER
Oh, yes, George Bailey. Whose ship has finally come in, if he has the sense to board it.

GEORGE
(chuckles as he begins to enjoy the thought of this windfall and settles back somewhat.)
(his eyes begin to wander a bit as he pictures in his mind what this means)
(tentatively, but importantly as he furrows his brow a bit while returning eye contact to Potter) Well, what happens to Bailey?

POTTER
Oh, confound it, man, are you afraid of success? I'm offering you a three year contract at three hundred thousand a year, starting today. Is it a deal or isn't it?

GEORGE
(his eyes dart about between looking at Potter and looking down conflicted, not wanting to blow this offer, but something in the back of his mind is uneasy) Well, Mr. Potter, I... I... I know I ought to jump at the chance, but I... I just... (leaning in a bit and looking at Potter as if hoping what he's going to ask is reasonable) I wonder if it would be possible for you to give me twenty-four hours to think it over?

POTTER
(jumping on George's last line after George barely finished the last word. Kindly and reasonably.) Sure, sure, sure. (encouraging) You go on home and talk about it to your wife.

GEORGE
(nodding in agreement) I'd like to do that.

POTTER
In the meantime, I'll draw up the papers.

GEORGE
(nodding once, more quietly and with respect) All right, sir.

POTTER
(offers hand)
Okay, George?

GEORGE
(smiling with a slight incredulous chuckle, pleased, as he shakes Potter's hand eagerly)
Okay, Mr. Potter.

(As they shake hands, George first feels confident, followed by a dawning physical revulsion as if Potter's hand feels like a cold mackerel to him. In that moment of physical contact he looks down at his hand as if a slimy imprint has been made, back at Potter suspiciously, and back at his hand again as he realizes he could never be associated with Potter. George drops his hand with a shudder. He peers intently into Potter's face as if trying to read the sinister intent behind Potter's eyes.)

GEORGE
(partly to himself and partly to Potter as the reality dawns on him) No... no... no... no, now wait a minute, here! (vehemently) I don't have to talk to anybody! (George's vehimence and anger is building) I know right now, and the answer is no! (explosively) NO! Damn it!
(angrily, yelling, gesturing at Potter)
You sit around here and you spin your little webs and you think the whole world revolves around you and your money. (forcefully as he leans into Potter's space) Well, it doesn't, Mr. Potter! In the... (gesturing madly) in the whole vast configuration of things, I'd say you were nothing but a (pinching his thumb and forefinger forcefully towards Potter as if holding a tiny spider he wants to crush) scurvy little spider.
