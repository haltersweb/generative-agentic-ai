## Assembly and preparation

Use the accepted previous end frame when the composition matches; inspect it for identity or hand drift before reuse. The previous square office portrait is a visual reference, not the correct start state for every clip. R01a needs the iPad on the desk; R01b picks it up; R02a/b retain it; R02c puts it down. Thereafter it stays on the desk. R10 requires a wider square view with hands and standing headroom; preserve that view through R13b. New pose/prop starting frames have not been generated.

Trim listening handles between parts of a continuous sentence, especially R07a/b, R08a/b, R12a/b and R13a/b. R08b ends on the question, and R09a begins immediately on Potter's reply. Keep voice, room tone, gesture state and breath plausible across every join. Timing direction may need adjustment after listening to generated audio. Never fake the handshake transformation with a cut.

The actor is permitted to look, lean, point, lift the iPad and rise as directed. The camera stays fixed. When Potter speaks, George's lips stay gently closed and silent; his eyes and breathing can react. Only the specified Potter hand enters R01a and R10; he remains completely outside all other frames.

Story continuity: $300,000, iPad, Monday then today exactly as written. Age 34, clean-shaven Daniel with neat side part. His objective is recognition and a real chance at success; his refusal begins in his body, and his anger discovers its words afterward.
